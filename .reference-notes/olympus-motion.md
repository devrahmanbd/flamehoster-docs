# Olympus reference motion notes

Observed on 2026-08-14 from https://theolympus.org/.

- Dark near-black page with thin, low-contrast grid lines and a subtle blue/purple atmospheric layer.
- Header is compact and floats over the background; controls use small restrained outline pills.
- Hero uses oversized gradient headline, asymmetric two-column composition, and dark glass cards with soft rounded borders.
- Cards feel interactive through bright, localized borders and arrow/action affordances rather than constant full-surface glow.
- The page is visually cinematic because depth comes from layered gradients, thin rules, soft shadows, and low-opacity geometry—not a saturated neon wash.
- Adaptation for Brick Docs: keep documentation text stable and readable; use animated grid drift/scan, route transition veil, pointer trail, and localized cyan edge response only on interactive surfaces. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and do not copy proprietary assets.
